The finding
Sealand · Content brief · September 2026
People ask to be shown what fits, pockets and how the bag carries

Shoppers ask what a bag actually takes in a normal day. Commenters name pockets, laptop space, water bottle holders, luggage pass through, size differences between models and the features included.

Buyers ask to see the bag in use, not only named. People also ask for the missing shopping basics, such as brand, price, buying links and adjustable parts.

In practice
Buyers frame one bag against work, school and travel, not one setting.
Commenters ask for carry comfort and pocket layout alongside brand, price and links.
Heard in August and September
What it means for what to make

People do not treat function as a list on a spec card. Comments want the bag turned into a visible shopping decision, what it carries, how it is organised, and how it sits on a body in work, school and travel terms. The missing basics around price and where to buy sit in the same picture.
