The finding
Sealand · Leadership brief · September 2026
Patagonia and Cotopaxi are chosen with repair and warranty in mind

Buyers pick Patagonia for an everyday bag, a travel bag and a casual outdoor bag from a maker they trust. People tie that trust to product quality, cleaner supply chains, environmental values and customer care. Repair and warranty expectations add reassurance around the choice.

Buyers pick Cotopaxi for travel and daily carry that feels colourful, lightweight, practical and durable. People connect the brand with scrap-based materials, playful design, repairability and a lifetime warranty.

In practice
Buyers tie repairability to travel and daily carry bags, not only outdoor gear.
People treat warranty and repair as part of trust in a brand, alongside colour, weight and durability.
Heard in August and September
What it means for the business

Patagonia and Cotopaxi show that after-sale care sits inside the buying case, not outside it. Repair and warranty help turn trust in a brand's values and product quality into a bag people feel safe choosing for daily use and travel.

Sealand · Leadership brief · September 2026
The risks, in business terms
Risk 1
Durability doubts can cut trust

Buyers ask whether a bag can take heavy loads, and failures under load send them to tougher fabrics or a maker they trust.

Argued in full in the Sales brief
Risk 2
Style can block eco bags

Buyers reject eco and upcycled bags when the look reads ugly, tacky, awkward, cheap or not luxurious.

Argued in full in the Marketing brief
